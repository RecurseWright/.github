# RecurseWright

RecurseWright is an evolving human–AI engineering system.

It explores what happens when people, AI, tools, methods, and engineering practice change together — while evidence, judgment, continuity, and human autonomy still need to remain visible.

It is not a fixed methodology.

It is developed through engineering work, experiments, artifacts, writing, and repeated re-evaluation.

## Artifacts

RecurseWright artifacts are small executable or interactive pieces of an investigation.

They are not demonstrations of a finished framework. They are ways to expose assumptions, make decisions observable, and create new evidence.

## The working principle

A RecurseWright claim is not protected by authorship.

It survives by evidence.

The same applies to methods, tools, artifacts, and RecurseWright itself.

---

## Current state

RecurseWright is being built in public.

The articles, artifacts, practices, tools, and even the current boundaries of the project are expected to evolve.

What should not disappear in that evolution is the human capacity to judge, question, revise, and leave.
